Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Cancelling a Booking

You can cancel an order yourself from "My Orders" any time before the assigned professional has started work. Once a job's status moves to "Started" or later, self-service cancellation is no longer available — contact support for exceptional circumstances (for example, a professional who never arrived).

2. Refunds Are Never Automatic

Every refund request is reviewed by a member of our support team before any money moves — there is no auto-refund trigger, even for online payments. This is intentional: it lets us weigh the specifics of what happened (partial work completed, quality issue, genuine cancellation, etc.) rather than applying a blanket rule.

3. How to Raise a Request

Open the order in "My Orders" → "Payment Details" → "Raise a Refund Request," describe what went wrong, and attach any supporting evidence (photos, etc.) if relevant. If a professional was assigned to the job, they may be asked to respond before our team makes a final decision.

4. Possible Outcomes

Depending on the situation, an approved request results in one of the following — decided case by case, never combined arbitrarily:

💰 Wallet Credit

The default outcome for most approved refunds — credited to your Remont Wallet for use on a future order.

🔀 Partial Wallet + Gateway

Used when part of the order is retained (e.g. materials already used) and part is refunded.

🏦 Full Gateway Refund

Reserved for exceptional cases — only available where the original payment was made online through our payment gateway, not for cash-collected orders.

🔧 Free Rework

The same professional (or another Partner) redoes the job at no extra cost — common for workmanship-quality issues, covered by our 30-day warranty.

🎟️ Discount Coupon

A coupon toward a future booking, used in cases where a partial goodwill gesture is appropriate.

🚫 No Refund

Used where the request doesn't meet the policy — for example, the service was completed as agreed and no issue is substantiated.

5. Cash-Collected Orders

If your order was paid for in cash, UPI, or card at the door ("Cash on Service/Delivery") rather than through our online payment gateway, an approved refund is issued as Remont Wallet credit rather than a gateway reversal, since there is no original online payment to reverse.

6. Timelines

Wallet credits are applied as soon as a decision is approved. Refunds routed back through the original payment gateway follow that provider's standard processing timelines, which are outside our direct control.

7. Product Returns

For physical products, contact support through the order's Payment Details within a reasonable period of delivery if the item is faulty, damaged, or materially different from what was ordered. The same review-then-decide process above applies, since a return may involve the product Vendor's response as well as ours.

8. Contact

For anything not covered here, reach us via Contact Us or email remont.care@gmail.com.
