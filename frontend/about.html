What We Do

Remont India is a marketplace, not a single contractor — every job is carried out by an independent, background-verified professional matched to your location and requirement. We cover home repair and maintenance services (AC, plumbing, electrical, appliance repair, cleaning), interior design and renovation, home and appliance products, and Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) that bundle recurring service visits at a fixed yearly price.

How a booking works

Describe your problem in Hindi, English, or Hinglish through our AI assistant, message us on WhatsApp, call directly, or book through the site — a verified professional nearby is matched and dispatched. Pricing is confirmed on-site before any work begins, and every completed order gets a GST-compliant invoice.
