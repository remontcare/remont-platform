Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started?▼
Tap "Book Consultation" to schedule a free call through the Remont booking system, or upload a room photo in the AI Studio tab above — a designer follows up either way.
Will I know the price before work starts?▼
Yes. Starting prices are shown upfront on this page; your designer confirms the exact, itemised cost after the site visit, and you approve it before any work begins.
Is the AI Design Studio free?▼
It's a small design fee (₹19 per design, or ₹49 for 3 designs) — this page is currently running in demo mode while the real payment and AI integration are being connected.
What does the warranty cover?▼
Every Complete Interior project carries a 10-year structural warranty. If a covered issue recurs within that period, it's fixed free of charge.
How long does a full home interior take?▼
Typically 45–90 days depending on scope, from design sign-off to handover. Your project manager shares a firm timeline after the site visit.
